## Lock Desk (lockdesk.co.uk)
Reader: UK city cyclists and commuters, often after a theft or buying a first good bike.
Worry: will it stop a thief, and for how long; is it too heavy to carry every day.
Their words: Sold Secure Gold/Silver/Diamond, angle grinder, bolt croppers, D-lock, chain, lock-up spot, frame and wheel.
Evidence they trust: security rating, shackle thickness, weight in kg, what it can lock to (railings, stands).
Examples: outside a station, an office rack, a flat hallway, a pub stop.
Avoid: fear-selling, "unbreakable" claims.
